# Governance

The documents that govern this system — the constitution, the development principles, the
organizational charter, and the rationality-monitoring design — are **held privately** and are not
published here.

That is a deliberate split rather than an omission. What this repository documents is *what the
system does and what it achieves*. How we think about building it, the guiding principles, and the
organizational model are internal.

## What this means when reading the rest of these documents

Other documents here reference the constitution and addenda 5, 11, 15 and 19 by name and section. Those
references are accurate and deliberately left in place — the governance structure is real and should
be visible even where its contents are not. A reference you cannot follow means the document is held
privately, not that it is missing.
