## Agent Organization, Training, Knowledge, Simulation, Alpha Flow, and Client Services

### About This Consolidation

This document consolidates the prior system constitution, client/server and agent architecture, continuous opportunity-detection flow, training/simulation design, portfolio-analysis design, and later architectural clarifications into one implementation-facing integration specification. Earlier domain specifications remain useful for detailed market logic and test cases. If an older organizational statement conflicts with an explicit clarification here, do not implement both. Reconcile the canonical specification and preserve one internally consistent rule.

### Current Bootstrap State

Bob/CEO is not yet implemented. Until Bob exists, the COO temporarily performs organizational bootstrap responsibilities that will later belong to Bob.

### 1. Foundational Principles

- The system is an agent-driven financial intelligence organization.
- The primary product is high-quality, non-obvious financial intelligence and trading-opportunity research, not automatic trade execution.
- Specialized agents have narrow responsibilities and communicate through structured contracts, reports, queues, and persistent organizational memory.
- Every meaningful unit of work is observable, attributable, gradable, and capable of feeding learning.
- Evidence precedes narrative. Deterministic methods perform known quantitative detection where appropriate; LLM reasoning handles interpretation, synthesis, ambiguity, and deeper analysis.
- Agents are replaceable. Validated organizational knowledge is durable.
- The system should remain quiet when nothing meaningful is happening. Activity is not intelligence.

### 2. Bootstrap Authority Model

- Current phase: server starts; Controller Agent starts; Controller creates COO as the first executive agent.
- Because Bob is not yet implemented, COO temporarily performs the organizational bootstrap responsibilities that Bob will later own.
- COO determines which organizational agents are required and requests their creation through Controller.
- Controller is the exclusive runtime creator and lifecycle executor. Other agents request lifecycle actions; they do not directly instantiate agents.
- Future phase: once Bob exists, Controller creates Bob first. Bob becomes CEO and organizational brain. COO returns to operational management; Controller remains lifecycle executor.

### 3. Initial Organization Before Bob

- Explorer Analyst, Speculator Analyst, and Analysis Analyst.
- Explorer Trainer, Speculator Trainer, and Analysis Trainer.
- HR Agent.
- Executive Trainer for management and executive roles.
- Independent evaluation capability for functions that would otherwise become self-certifying.
- Banker Agents created on demand for client sessions.
- Portfolio Analysis Agents created or allocated on demand.
- Explorer, Speculator, and Analysis are three analyst specialties within one broader analysis organization. They share the mission of producing traceable opportunity intelligence but retain distinct responsibilities.

### 4. Universal Agent Lifecycle

- Create -> train -> validate -> operate -> evaluate -> improve/remediate -> suspend/retire -> resume -> revalidate.
- Retirement means serialized dormancy with state preserved. It is not destruction.
- Destructive deletion is separate, explicit, exceptional, and rare.
- Health degradation triggers evidence-based diagnosis and remediation.
- Performance history, lessons, and validated knowledge survive process restart or retirement.

### 5. HR, Training, And Evaluation

- HR owns training standards, capability development, and organizational health.
- HR manages Training Agents and communicates health/performance findings and remediation recommendations.
- Training Agents design scenarios, administer exercises, assess results, provide reinforcement/correction, and capture validated lessons.
- One Training Agent may initially perform test development, simulation orchestration, grading, feedback, and knowledge capture, but those functions remain logically separable.
- Every agent is trainable and evaluable, including COO, HR, trainers, evaluators, Controller, and later Bob.
- No evaluator self-certifies. HR and other evaluation functions must themselves be independently evaluated.

### 6. Competency-Driven Training

- Training begins with skills, not arbitrary tests.
- Each role has a competency/skill catalog.
- Each skill defines purpose, success criteria, failure modes, difficulty progression, and evidence required for certification.
- Each skill maps to simulation templates or test scenarios.
- Every test traces to a competency; every competency should eventually have measurable, repeatable tests.
- Certification requires repeatable performance across varied scenarios, including noise, boundary cases, missing data, contradictions, and adversarial conditions.

### 7. Training Stages

- Stage 1: Synthetic simulation with controlled generated data and hidden ground truth.
- Stage 2: Historical replay through the same interfaces without look-ahead.
- Stage 3: Live observation / paper research without automatic trade execution.
- Stage 4: Production promotion only after required gates are satisfied.
- Real-world outcomes feed back into curriculum, regression scenarios, simulation fidelity, and confidence calibration.

### 8. Organizational Knowledge

- Knowledge must be persisted as organizational data, not left only in transient agent context.
- The database is the canonical source of truth for validated lessons, tests, corrections, FAQs, rationale, training history, and performance evidence.
- Initial knowledge classification has two layers only.
- Resident knowledge is stored in the database and also loaded/embedded with an agent because it should be immediately available.
- Database-only knowledge is stored durably and retrieved on demand.
- Resident knowledge is a working representation, not a competing source of truth.

### 9. Persistence Implementation Requirement

- Pre-Alpha task: put persistence behind a stable data-access / knowledge-service abstraction.
- SQLite may remain the initial database.
- Agents must not depend directly on SQLite-specific behavior.
- The abstraction must allow later migration to PostgreSQL with minimal changes to agent logic.
- PostgreSQL plus pgvector is a suitable future path if semantic retrieval becomes necessary.
- Caches may improve speed but must never become the only durable copy of organizational knowledge.

### 10. Pre-Alpha Static Metadata

- Create a diverse global Agent Name Repository. Every active agent also has an immutable internal identifier.
- The CEO display name is a configurable reserved-name setting whose initial/default value is Bob. Bob must not be hard-coded throughout application logic.
- Create a configurable, expandable, versioned Security Universe of assets the organization may monitor.
- Inclusion in the universe means the asset may be monitored; it does not imply that it is attractive or investable.
- Agents may reach strongly negative or contrarian conclusions when supported by evidence. Evaluation judges rigor, evidence, calibration, and traceability rather than popularity.

### 11. Simulation Engine

- Simulation starts from the learning objective, not from arbitrary fake data.
- Training defines the skill and conditions to test; the Simulation Engine generates the data required to exercise that skill.
- Each scenario carries hidden ground truth, expected behaviors, scoring criteria, seed/run identity, provenance, and reproducibility information.
- Simulation models should be pluggable so established market-simulation models can be selected rather than requiring every model to be invented internally.
- Pre-Alpha includes an initial simulation engine and scenario library sufficient to drive the entire Alpha organization.

### 12. Explorer Analyst Skills

- Construct implied-volatility surfaces for assigned securities from the provided data interface.
- Analyze volatility surfaces using deterministic/statistical methods.
- Identify local anomalies/dislocations and distinguish meaningful signals from ordinary variation and noise.
- Compare individual-security behavior with explicit peer groups and classify common versus idiosyncratic events.
- Produce structured evidence with provenance, confidence, magnitude, and reproducible detector context.
- When a meaningful anomaly is found, request contextual corroboration from Speculator through a defined contract.

### 13. Speculator Analyst Skills

- Monitor simulated or real external chatter/context sources for unusual attention, rumors, narratives, or information velocity.
- Separate useful signal from irrelevant chatter, coordinated noise, false leads, rumor, and popularity without substance.
- Corroborate meaningful claims across multiple sources where possible.
- Track provenance, information velocity, confidence, novelty, and corroboration.
- Treat chatter as evidence, not truth.
- When meaningful contextual evidence appears, request quantitative corroboration from Explorer through the explicit contract.

### 14. Explorer-Speculator Collaboration

- Investigate independently first, then cross-check.
- Explorer may originate a lead from quantitative evidence and ask Speculator for contextual corroboration.
- Speculator may originate a lead from contextual evidence and ask Explorer for quantitative corroboration.
- Requests and responses contain evidence, confidence, provenance/lineage, timestamps, and the specific question being answered.
- Disagreement is preserved rather than erased.
- A formal lead normally requires a configurable escalation rule based on the strength and compatibility of combined evidence.
- The exact consensus threshold should be calibrated through testing rather than permanently hard-coded.

### 15. Analysis Analyst

- Receives the joint evidence packet after discovery escalation.
- Performs deeper reasoning using quantitative evidence, peer context, contextual evidence, uncertainty, historical context when available, and other permitted information.
- May conclude: irrelevant, insufficient evidence, meaningful risk, potential opportunity, or exceptional opportunity.
- Produces a client-facing opportunity report with thesis, evidence, uncertainty, confidence, competing explanations, and reasons the idea could be wrong.
- Assigns an opportunity grade with traceable rationale.
- Grades upstream Explorer/Speculator work; later market outcomes grade Analysis and confidence calibration.

### 16. End-To-End Training Sequence

- Train each specialty in isolation first.
- Then train Explorer-Speculator collaboration and handoff behavior.
- Then train the full discovery-to-analysis pipeline.
- Finally run the entire organization continuously under simulated day-to-day conditions.
- The synthetic environment should resemble a working market day: data arrives, analysts observe, candidates are investigated, evidence is cross-checked, reports are filed, Analysis evaluates them, and ordinary events quietly produce no escalation.

### 17. Alpha Definition

- Alpha is the full organization operating end-to-end on simulated data for an extended period without systemic failure and while meeting defined performance milestones.
- Alpha is not merely one detector demonstration.
- Controller, acting COO, HR, trainers, Explorer, Speculator, Analysis, Banker/client flow, Portfolio Analysis, persistence, queues, evaluation, and organizational memory should operate together.
- The simulated environment continuously generates realistic work so the organization behaves as though it were observing markets day to day.
- The system must demonstrate stable continuous operation, correct routing, recoverability, persistent grades and knowledge, role-boundary compliance, and measurable quality.
- Historical-data certification follows Alpha.

### 18. Banker Agent And Client Interface

- Banker is the trusted general-purpose client representative.
- When a client logs in, Controller creates or restores a Banker Agent according to lifecycle policy.
- The client talks to Banker, not directly to specialist analytical agents.
- Banker coordinates internal services, requests Portfolio Analysis or other capabilities, explains results, and maintains relationship continuity.
- Banker is a relationship/orchestration role, not a substitute for Portfolio Analysis or the analyst team.
- Banker processes may be transient, but client relationship knowledge is persistent.

### 19. Client Relationship Memory

- Before interacting with a client, Banker loads relevant relationship memory into working context.
- Relationship memory may include prior conversations, preferences, objectives, communication preferences, client notes, prior analyses, decisions, and unresolved questions.
- Conversation records must support provenance and auditability.
- The system may maintain inferred or unexpressed client objectives as hypotheses, but facts and inferences must be clearly separated.
- Every inference carries evidence/provenance and confidence. Low-confidence hunches are never silently promoted to fact.
- When an inferred need may help the client, Banker asks a clarifying question and updates the relationship record based on the response.
- Proactive clarification is preferred over assumption.

### 20. Portfolio Analysis Agent

- Portfolio Analysis remains separate from continuous market discovery.
- Banker requests Portfolio Analysis on behalf of the client.
- Portfolio Analysis combines supplied portfolio information into a unified analytical view and may use relevant organizational research/opportunity information.
- Initial default analysis covers holdings, concentrations, overlapping exposures, directional/issuer/sector exposure, diversification, option positions/basic option exposure, obvious risks, and relevant opportunities when useful.
- The client can customize or extend the requested analysis beyond the default workflow.
- Portfolio Analysis returns its report through Banker and is itself trained, evaluated, and subject to the universal lifecycle.

### 21. Pre-Alpha Task List

- Create the global Agent Name Repository and configurable reserved CEO name.
- Create the versioned Security Universe.
- Implement the persistence/knowledge abstraction over SQLite.
- Define common agent identity, lifecycle, task, evidence, report, grade, outcome, and health contracts.
- Define initial skill catalogs for Explorer, Speculator, Analysis, COO, trainers, Banker, and Portfolio Analysis as required for Alpha.
- Build the pluggable Simulation Engine interface and initial synthetic scenario libraries.
- Implement role-specific Training Agents and grading/feedback persistence.

### 22. Alpha Operating Flow

1. Start server/runtime.
2. Start Controller Agent.
3. Controller creates acting COO.
4. Acting COO requests creation of HR, trainers, analysts, evaluators, and required capabilities.
5. Controller creates requested agents.
6. Static metadata and organizational knowledge are loaded.
7. Training Agents use Simulation Engine to train and certify role competencies.
8. Synthetic market begins continuous operation.
9. Explorer constructs/analyzes volatility surfaces and searches for quantitative anomalies.
10. Speculator monitors synthetic chatter/context and searches for meaningful contextual signals.
11. Explorer and Speculator independently investigate and cross-check meaningful leads through structured contracts.
12. Qualified evidence is filed as a structured lead/report.
13. Analysis performs deep evaluation, produces the opportunity assessment, and grades upstream work.
14. Grades, outcomes, lessons, and performance records persist and feed training.
15. Client login creates/restores Banker. Banker may request Portfolio Analysis and other client services.
16. Portfolio Analysis uses client portfolio data plus relevant organizational intelligence and returns the result through Banker.
17. The organization continues this loop for the full Alpha duration while health, quality, throughput, failures, recoveries, and knowledge persistence are measured.

### 23. Explicitly Deferred

- Bob/CEO implementation and permanent CEO-first startup sequence.
- Final economic objective function for Bob.
- Large-scale multi-host orchestration.
- Automatic production self-modification without promotion gates.
- Knowledge tiers beyond the initial two-layer model.
- Final PostgreSQL/pgvector migration timing.
- Automatic trade execution and order management.
- Detailed portfolio optimization, rebalancing, tax-lot, and institutional risk-platform functionality.
- Permanent consensus thresholds and advanced simulation-model selection policy; these should be calibrated from evidence.

### 24. Canonical Implementation Invariants

- The architecture is agent-driven.
- Only Controller physically creates or changes runtime lifecycle state.
- Before Bob exists, COO temporarily performs bootstrap CEO responsibilities. After Bob exists, Controller creates Bob first and Bob assumes CEO authority.
- Every organizational role is trainable and evaluable.
- Every meaningful piece of work produces evaluation evidence.
- Knowledge belongs to the organization and persists independently of transient agents.
- SQLite is acceptable initially, but persistence must be database-abstracted and migration-ready.
- Pre-Alpha establishes metadata, skills, simulation, persistence, and contracts.
- Alpha is the complete organization running continuously on simulated data.
- Explorer, Speculator, and Analysis are analyst specialties with explicit handoffs and preserved independent reasoning.
- Banker owns the client relationship interface; Portfolio Analysis remains a separate analytical capability.
- Facts and client inferences are never conflated.
- Research conclusions are judged by evidence and calibration, not conformity.
- Continuous learning is allowed; production capability changes remain validation-gated.
- When requirements conflict, do not silently preserve contradictory alternatives. Reconcile the canonical specification.
