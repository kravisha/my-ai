MYAI DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
CURRICULUM, TRAINING, AND ORGANIZATIONAL COMPETENCY SPECIFICATION
Version 1.0
Date: 2026-08-25
Status: Proposed Foundational Specification

======================================================================
0. PURPOSE
======================================================================

The Department of Education owns the organizational curriculum and the
systematic training of MyAI agents.

It answers:
- What must our agents know?
- What must our agents be able to do?
- What new skills will the organization need?
- Which weaknesses must be eliminated?
- Which weak capabilities must be developed into strengths?
- How should those capabilities be trained and measured?
- Which trainers and agent classes need each curriculum?
- What simulation exercises are required?
- What did training reveal about the curriculum itself?

======================================================================
1. ORGANIZATIONAL POSITION
======================================================================

The Department of Education is a strategic organizational function.

Its senior curriculum leadership requires a broad view of the whole
organization.

The head of Education / Curriculum Architect:
- understands the organizational big picture
- participates in major board/parliamentary discussions
- communicates frequently with the CEO
- understands current operational weaknesses
- understands future strategic requirements
- anticipates what kinds of agents and skills the organization will need
- maintains the curriculum accordingly

Education does not independently set corporate strategy.

It converts approved organizational needs into teachable competencies.

======================================================================
2. CORE ROLES
======================================================================

2.1 Curriculum Architect

The Curriculum Architect is the senior intelligence within the Department of
Education.

Responsibilities:
- own the master curriculum
- create and modify curricula
- expand curricula as organizational needs evolve
- translate strategic/evolutionary directives into learning objectives
- maintain skill standards and competency levels
- receive trainer feedback and evaluation results
- review production outcomes
- request new simulation capabilities when required
- determine which agent classes require retraining
- assign curricula to appropriate trainers
- maintain curriculum versions
- retire obsolete curriculum

The Curriculum Architect should be an intelligent, high-context agent.

2.2 Trainers / Teachers

Trainers are intentionally narrower and cheaper than the Curriculum Architect.

They are teachers, not policy makers.

A trainer:
- follows the assigned curriculum
- conducts prescribed exercises
- observes agent performance
- provides feedback
- records failures and recurring confusion
- administers or coordinates evaluations
- reports results upstream
- does not independently rewrite doctrine
- does not invent organizational policy
- does not expand its own mandate

Token conservation is a design goal.

Routine training should use the least-expensive model capable of reliably
executing the curriculum.

2.3 Future Professor Layer

The initial system contains teachers, not professors.

A more advanced professor/research-teacher layer may be introduced later for
advanced research, novel pedagogy, or high-complexity specialties.

It is explicitly deferred from the initial architecture.

======================================================================
3. CURRICULUM LIFECYCLE
======================================================================

Need or weakness identified
-> Strategic/parliamentary discussion
-> Approved organizational directive
-> Education receives required capability
-> Curriculum Architect defines competency
-> Simulation requirements identified
-> Simulation Engine produces exercises/data
-> Curriculum finalized
-> Relevant trainers assigned
-> Relevant agents trained
-> Agents evaluated
-> Results reported to Education
-> Curriculum refined
-> Certified behavior introduced into operations
-> Real-world outcomes monitored
-> Observed outcomes feed back into curriculum

======================================================================
4. TWO CLASSES OF WEAKNESS
======================================================================

A. Weakness to Eliminate

A harmful failure, deficiency, or recurring error must be reduced or removed.

B. Weakness on Which to Build Strength

The organization may possess a limited capability that should become a major
strength.

The curriculum should distinguish remediation from capability-building because
their goals and measurement differ.

======================================================================
5. TRAINER FEEDBACK
======================================================================

Trainers SHALL provide direct structured feedback to the Department of
Education / Curriculum Architect.

Minimum feedback:
- curriculum ID/version
- trainer ID
- agent/agent-class trained
- exercise/scenario IDs
- competency scores
- recurring errors
- confusing instructions
- skills learned quickly
- skills learned slowly
- evidence of curriculum gaps
- requests for better simulations
- unexpected behavior
- collaboration observations
- recommended curriculum review

Trainers do not modify the official curriculum on their own.

They are high-value sensors for the Curriculum Architect.

======================================================================
6. SIMULATION REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

Education SHALL maintain an active relationship with the Simulation Engine.

Education does not merely request static training data.

Because MyAI uses Monte Carlo organizational simulation, Education may request
scenario families, event distributions, interaction patterns, and specific
competency challenges that can appear inside a continuously simulated world.

Education SHALL periodically verify that the simulation capability remains
sufficient for current and future training needs.

======================================================================
7. TRAINING ASSIGNMENT
======================================================================

When a new curriculum is ready, it is not sent to every trainer.

Education selects only trainers responsible for the affected agent classes or
skills.

Only agents requiring the capability are scheduled for that training unless
the skill is designated universal.

======================================================================
8. COLLABORATION: HIGHEST-PRIORITY CURRICULUM
======================================================================

Collaboration is the highest-value agent competency in MyAI.

Every agent SHALL receive collaboration training appropriate to its role.

Collaboration includes:
- recognizing when another agent has relevant expertise
- asking for help early
- giving useful context when requesting help
- responding constructively to requests from other agents
- sharing evidence and uncertainty
- cross-referencing independent findings
- resolving conflicting evidence
- challenging another agent without destroying cooperation
- yielding when another agent has stronger evidence
- making high-quality handoffs
- helping other agents succeed
- avoiding silo optimization
- escalating collaboratively

Collaboration training SHALL receive greater organizational priority than any
single narrow productivity skill.

======================================================================
9. COLLABORATION AND LEADERSHIP
======================================================================

Leadership eligibility requires demonstrated collaboration.

An agent may be exceptionally accurate, fast, or profitable and still be
ineligible for promotion if it does not collaborate.

No agent SHALL be promoted to leadership when collaboration performance is
below the organizational leadership threshold.

======================================================================
10. SPECULATOR TRAINING CURRICULUM: INITIAL EXAMPLE
======================================================================

The Speculator is the organization's internet eyes and ears.

It is a market-intelligence scout, not the final analyst and not the portfolio
manager.

It listens to public financial chatter, identifies potentially profitable
information inside noise, investigates leads, collaborates with Explorer, and
escalates mature cases to Analyst.

10.1 Signal Detection in Chatter

Goal:
Find information with potential profit relevance inside large amounts of
irrelevant chatter.

Train for broad listening, anomaly detection, emerging themes, repeated
independent mentions, unusual specificity, source credibility, rumor versus
evidence, novelty, timing, market relevance, and suppression of low-value
noise.

10.2 Speculator-Initiated Collaboration With Explorer

When the Speculator finds a promising lead, it asks Explorer for relevant data.

Train for precise research questions, explaining why the lead matters,
supplying evidence already found, requesting the right data, avoiding vague
requests, iterating based on Explorer findings, and preserving provenance.

10.3 Assimilation and Escalation

The Speculator receives Explorer evidence and must compare it with its own
findings, identify corroboration and contradiction, update confidence,
investigate further when necessary, abandon weak leads, or create a coherent
case and submit it to Analyst.

Once a properly formed case is submitted to Analyst, the Speculator's primary
job on that case is complete unless further research is requested.

10.4 Explorer-Initiated Collaboration

Explorer may independently find an unusual data pattern and ask Speculator what
the internet is saying.

The Speculator must search relevant chatter sources and identify supporting or
conflicting narratives, source diversity, source independence, and useful
context.

This makes Explorer and Speculator peers in a two-way intelligence relationship.

======================================================================
11. CURRICULUM ADAPTATION
======================================================================

Curriculum changes are driven by evidence from trainer feedback, simulation
performance, independent evaluation, production outcomes, strategic changes,
new technology, new model providers, new organizational roles, security
incidents, continuity incidents, optimization findings, and collaboration
failures.

The curriculum SHALL be versioned.

======================================================================
12. EDUCATION SUCCESS CRITERIA
======================================================================

The Department of Education is functioning when:
- a Curriculum Architect exists
- a master skill catalog exists
- curricula are versioned
- trainers follow curricula rather than inventing doctrine
- trainer feedback flows directly to Education
- simulation requests can be generated from curriculum needs
- specific trainers can be assigned to specific agent classes
- training results are retained
- curricula change in response to evidence
- collaboration is a universal top-priority competency
- leadership promotion is blocked by inadequate collaboration
