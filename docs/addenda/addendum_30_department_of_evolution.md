MYAI DEPARTMENT OF EVOLUTION
Architecture and Engineering Specification
Version 2.0
Date: 2026-08-23
Status: Foundational Design Specification

======================================================================
0. DOCUMENT PURPOSE
======================================================================

This document defines the Department of Evolution for MyAI.

Version 2.0 clarifies the boundary between Strategy and Evolution.

The Department of Evolution does NOT decide which wants or needs deserve
organizational priority.

That function belongs to the Strategy Department.

Evolution receives approved strategic mandates and transforms them into safe,
testable, trained, certified, staged systemic change.

Evolution owns:

- evolution directives
- behavioral metadata
- training
- assimilation
- evaluation
- certification
- version transition
- rollout
- rollback
- controlled architecture evolution

======================================================================
1. THREE DISTINCT CONCEPTS
======================================================================

1.1 Agentic Learning

Individual agent learning inside its existing mission, authority, and
behavioral envelope.

It does not automatically change the system.

1.2 Systemic Learning

Cross-agent and cross-department learning that identifies patterns about the
system.

It informs Strategy and Evolution.

1.3 Systemic Evolution

A deliberate change to system-wide or department-wide behavior, architecture,
interfaces, capability contracts, orchestration, or governance.

Systemic Evolution is executed by the Department of Evolution only after
appropriate strategic authorization.

======================================================================
2. SOURCES OF EVOLUTION MANDATES
======================================================================

Systemic Evolution MAY originate from:

- Need-based strategic resolution
- Want-based strategic resolution
- Security remediation requiring architectural change
- Business Continuity remediation
- Board resolution
- constitutional amendment
- regulatorily required change
- approved provider migration
- approved architecture modernization

Except for emergency containment, these SHALL pass through Strategy before
Evolution.

======================================================================
3. NEED-BASED AND WANT-BASED EXECUTION
======================================================================

Evolution SHALL preserve the Strategy classification.

NEED-BASED:
    typically higher urgency
    may receive compressed timelines
    may reserve resources
    may receive elevated Board visibility

WANT-BASED:
    optional strategic improvement
    proceeds according to approved priority and available capacity
    may be deferred or canceled

Evolution SHALL not independently convert a Want into a Need.

======================================================================
4. EVOLUTION DIRECTIVE
======================================================================

Every approved systemic evolution SHALL be translated into a versioned
Evolution Directive.

Required metadata:

- directive ID
- source Strategy resolution
- Need/Want classification
- priority
- urgency flag
- scope
- affected departments
- affected agent classes
- required behavioral outcome
- training requirements
- evaluation criteria
- certification criteria
- compatibility constraints
- rollout plan
- rollback plan
- effective version
- communication channel

======================================================================
5. DIRECTIVE COMMUNICATION AGENT
======================================================================

Every major directive SHALL have a Directive Communication Agent.

It SHALL:

- explain the directive
- answer agent questions
- clarify ambiguity
- publish canonical examples
- record recurring confusion
- coordinate amendments
- preserve a single authoritative interpretation

======================================================================
6. TRAINER ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

Central trainer roles:

- Chief Evolution Trainer
- Directive Trainer
- Assimilation Trainer
- Simulation Trainer
- Cross-Department Trainer
- Emergency Retraining Trainer

Department-specific trainers MAY operate under central certification rules.

Trainers SHALL NOT:

- expand their own authority
- certify themselves
- conceal failed evaluations
- rewrite constitutional policy
- bypass Strategy authorization
- deploy unapproved behavior

======================================================================
7. AGENT ASSIMILATION
======================================================================

An agent SHALL normally assimilate new behavior as follows:

ACTIVE
-> NOTIFIED
-> DRAINING
-> CHECKPOINTING
-> TRAINING
-> EVALUATING
-> CERTIFIED
-> RESTARTING / RESUMING
-> REJOINING
-> ACTIVE_NEW_VERSION

Agents SHOULD complete or safely hand off current work before training.

======================================================================
8. LOAD-AWARE EVOLUTION
======================================================================

Evolution SHALL not unnecessarily disrupt day-to-day operations.

Training SHALL preferentially occur during low-load windows.

The Evolution Scheduler SHALL consider:

- current utilization
- predicted utilization
- queue depth
- spare agent capacity
- service criticality
- minimum active capacity
- training duration
- rollout risk

Large evolutions SHALL proceed in waves.

======================================================================
9. CAPACITY FLOOR
======================================================================

Every department SHALL define a minimum operational capacity floor.

The Evolution Scheduler SHALL not intentionally reduce active capacity below
that floor except under explicitly approved emergency conditions.

======================================================================
10. ROLE OF COO
======================================================================

COO is operational coordinator.

COO SHALL:

- schedule agent draining
- maintain operational capacity
- coordinate handoffs
- sequence restarts
- coordinate with Gateway
- monitor rollout progress

COO does not own:

- strategic priority
- evolution justification
- directive content
- trainer doctrine
- certification rules

======================================================================
11. GATEWAY AGENT
======================================================================

The Gateway is the external interface to MyAI.

During evolution it MAY:

- route around agents in training
- buffer eligible work
- apply backpressure
- perform version-aware routing
- reduce intake
- preserve client continuity

Gateway is a top-level infrastructure component, protected by Security but not
owned by Security.

======================================================================
12. METADATA-DRIVEN BEHAVIORAL CHANGE
======================================================================

Behavioral evolution SHOULD prefer:

- metadata
- directives
- policy
- capability configuration
- versioned behavioral packages

before requiring code changes.

Code changes are justified when metadata cannot safely express the required
system behavior.

======================================================================
13. NO GLOBAL SERIALIZATION REQUIREMENT
======================================================================

MyAI is not a single monolithic object that must be serialized and restarted.

Systemic state is distributed across:

- agents
- services
- policies
- metadata
- data stores
- interfaces
- queues
- control planes

Evolution SHALL therefore occur through coordinated version transition.

======================================================================
14. ROLLING CODE EVOLUTION
======================================================================

Where code changes are required, prefer:

- rolling agent restart
- service-by-service deployment
- canary release
- blue/green release
- version coexistence
- compatibility adapters
- state transfer
- rollback

Full-system shutdown SHALL be a last resort.

======================================================================
15. COO EVOLUTION
======================================================================

Because COO is central to orchestration, COO replacement requires a special
handoff process.

Possible mechanisms:

- deputy COO
- shadow COO
- state transfer
- Gateway buffering
- staged authority transfer
- controlled restart of COO only

The rest of the system SHOULD remain operational.

======================================================================
16. EVALUATION
======================================================================

Every evolution SHALL define measurable success criteria.

Evaluation MAY include:

- comprehension
- task quality
- reliability
- safety
- security
- regression
- latency
- cost
- compatibility
- operational impact
- failure handling

======================================================================
17. CERTIFICATION
======================================================================

Certification states:

PENDING
IN_TRAINING
EVALUATING
CERTIFIED
CONDITIONALLY_CERTIFIED
FAILED
RETRAIN_REQUIRED
QUARANTINED
RETIRED

Agents requiring evolution SHALL not regain full production authority until
appropriately certified.

======================================================================
18. PREVENTING OVERZEALOUS TRAINERS AND EVALUATORS
======================================================================

No trainer, evaluator, certifier, or judge is infallible.

Where practical:

- trainer trains
- evaluator evaluates
- certifier certifies
- auditor audits

Negative judgments SHALL cite evidence.

Appeal and review SHALL exist.

A single evaluator SHALL NOT permanently retire or destroy an agent.

======================================================================
19. GOVERNANCE REVIEW
======================================================================

An independent Governance Review function SHALL monitor:

- trainer behavior
- evaluator consistency
- certification fairness
- systemic bias
- mission drift
- unjustified restrictions
- appeal outcomes

======================================================================
20. ROGUE AGENTS
======================================================================

Rogue Agent means an agent that materially departs from certified behavior,
authority, or directive.

Ordinary rogue behavior MAY be handled through Law Enforcement, retraining,
restriction, probation, or retirement.

Malicious corruption is different.

======================================================================
21. MALICIOUS CORRUPTION
======================================================================

If malicious compromise is credibly suspected, Security Defense MAY
immediately contain the agent.

Containment MAY include:

- isolation
- credential revocation
- network cutoff
- tool suspension
- communication freeze
- evidence preservation
- workload rerouting

Containment is protective, temporary, and auditable.

It is not punishment.

======================================================================

======================================================================
26. VERSIONED AGENT BEHAVIOR
======================================================================

Every production agent SHOULD expose:

- agent type
- runtime ID
- behavior version
- directive set
- certification status
- capability version
- training timestamp

This supports:

- audit
- mixed-version operation
- rollback
- targeted retraining
- compatibility checking

======================================================================
27. ROLLBACK
======================================================================

Rollback SHALL be defined before rollout.

Possible rollback targets:

- previous directive
- previous behavioral metadata
- previous capability package
- previous binary
- previous container
- previous provider
- previous routing rule

Failure evidence SHALL be preserved.

======================================================================
28. RELATIONSHIP TO STRATEGY
======================================================================

Strategy decides:

- Need/Want
- priority
- business value
- competitive value
- timing intent
- Board approval
- Development Plan placement

Evolution decides:

- technical evolution design
- directive
- training
- certification
- rollout
- rollback

Evolution SHALL NOT bypass Strategy for ordinary systemic change.

======================================================================
29. RELATIONSHIP TO SECURITY DEFENSE
======================================================================

Security may:

- contain compromised agents
- impose emergency restrictions
- block unsafe rollout
- require security remediation

Evolution may:

- retrain
- recertify
- redesign behavior
- implement approved systemic security change

======================================================================
30. RELATIONSHIP TO BUSINESS CONTINUITY
======================================================================

Business Continuity may delay or reshape rollout when minimum operational
resilience would be violated.

Evolution SHALL coordinate:

- spare capacity
- rollback readiness
- agent availability
- service continuity
- recovery implications

======================================================================
31. RELATIONSHIP TO LAW ENFORCEMENT
======================================================================

Law Enforcement:
    ordinary rule violation

Security Defense:
    malicious compromise and containment

Evolution:
    retraining, recertification, systemic correction

These functions SHALL remain distinct.

======================================================================
32. CONSTITUTIONAL EVOLUTION DIRECTIVES
======================================================================

DIRECTIVE E1
Agentic Learning, Systemic Learning, Strategy, and Systemic Evolution are
distinct functions.

DIRECTIVE E2
No non-emergency systemic evolution SHALL bypass Strategy.

DIRECTIVE E3
Evolution SHALL execute approved Needs and Wants according to Strategy
priority.

DIRECTIVE E4
Evolution SHALL prefer metadata-driven behavioral adaptation before code
change when safe and sufficient.

DIRECTIVE E5
Evolution SHALL not normally require full-system shutdown.

DIRECTIVE E6
Agents SHALL assimilate change at safe operational boundaries.

DIRECTIVE E7
Training SHALL preferentially occur during low-load periods.

DIRECTIVE E8
Operational capacity SHALL remain above defined floors during routine
evolution.

DIRECTIVE E9
COO coordinates evolution but does not own strategy or evolution policy.

DIRECTIVE E10
Gateway SHALL support continuity during version transitions.

DIRECTIVE E11
No trainer, evaluator, or certifier is infallible.

DIRECTIVE E12
Major evolutionary authority SHALL be separated across training, evaluation,
certification, deployment, and audit.

DIRECTIVE E13
Rogue behavior and malicious corruption are distinct.

DIRECTIVE E14
Security Defense owns containment of malicious corruption.

DIRECTIVE E15
Containment is temporary, bounded, auditable, and protective.

DIRECTIVE E16
Every major evolution SHALL support rollback.

DIRECTIVE E17
Every agent SHALL expose a behavioral version and certification state.

======================================================================
33. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
======================================================================

Department of Evolution v2 is operational when:

- Strategy-to-Evolution mandates exist
- directives are versioned
- Communication Agents exist
- trainers exist
- independent evaluators exist
- certification exists
- agents can drain and rejoin
- load-aware rollout exists
- capacity floors exist
- COO coordination exists
- Gateway routing supports rollout
- rollback exists
- code evolution can roll without global shutdown
- Governance Review exists
- Security containment integrates correctly
