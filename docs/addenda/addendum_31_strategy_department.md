MYAI STRATEGY DEPARTMENT
Architecture and Governance Specification
Version 1.0
Date: 2026-08-23
Status: Foundational Design Specification

======================================================================
0. DOCUMENT PURPOSE
======================================================================

This document defines the MyAI Strategy Department.

The Strategy Department is a top-level organizational and architectural
division responsible for deciding WHAT the organization should pursue and in
what priority.

It does not implement systemic evolution directly.

It does not train agents.

It does not own routine operations.

It does not replace the Board, President, CEO, COO, Security Defense,
Business Continuity, or the Department of Evolution.

Its role is to transform needs, wants, external opportunities, risks,
departmental proposals, and long-term objectives into coherent strategic
priorities and approved development plans.

All proposed systemic evolution, whether need-based or want-based, SHALL pass
through the Strategy Department before implementation by the Department of
Evolution, except narrowly defined emergency containment actions owned by
Security Defense.

The Strategy Department therefore acts as the bridge between:

- what the system experiences
- what agents and departments want
- what the system urgently needs
- what the outside world is becoming
- what the Board and President decide
- what the Department of Evolution ultimately implements

======================================================================
1. CONSTITUTIONAL ROLE
======================================================================

1.1 Strategy Before Evolution

No non-emergency systemic evolution SHALL begin merely because an agent,
department, trainer, evaluator, executive, or external intelligence source
suggests it.

The proposal SHALL enter Strategy.

Strategy SHALL determine:

- whether the proposal is a NEED or a WANT
- the urgency
- the strategic value
- competing priorities
- resource requirements
- expected benefit
- opportunity cost
- risk
- timing
- whether the proposal belongs in the active Development Plan

1.2 Separation of Strategic Choice and Evolutionary Execution

Strategy decides:
    WHAT should be pursued and WHY.

Evolution decides:
    HOW approved systemic change is trained, assimilated, certified,
    versioned, rolled out, and reversed if necessary.

COO coordinates:
    WHEN and HOW operational capacity is scheduled during execution.

Board and President govern:
    WHICH major priorities are ultimately approved.

======================================================================
2. NEED-BASED AND WANT-BASED STRATEGY
======================================================================

2.1 Need-Based Items

A Need is a condition that materially threatens the ability of the system to
meet required objectives if left unresolved.

Examples:

- sustained performance deterioration
- repeated failure despite correct execution
- security weakness requiring architectural change
- continuity weakness
- legal or regulatory requirement
- scaling wall
- provider obsolescence
- unacceptable operating cost
- architectural constraint blocking essential work
- repeated agent failure that agentic learning cannot correct

Need-based items SHALL receive strong priority.

2.2 Need Priority Flags

Need-based items MAY carry escalation flags.

Suggested flags:

GREEN
    identified need, no immediate degradation

YELLOW
    measurable deterioration, action should be planned

ORANGE
    significant deterioration or growing exposure

RED
    threshold approaching unacceptable condition; action is urgent

CRITICAL
    immediate strategic action required, subject to Security/Continuity
    emergency authorities where applicable

A priority flag is not merely a label.

It SHALL influence:

- queue position
- review frequency
- resource reservation
- Board visibility
- Evolution readiness
- escalation requirements

2.3 Want-Based Items

A Want is a desirable improvement that is not presently required for safe or
acceptable operation.

Examples:

- better roads, metaphorically: better infrastructure
- more compute capacity
- faster response time where current speed is acceptable
- more automation
- improved user experience
- higher revenue
- improved profitability
- more model options
- better tools
- stronger analytics
- new capabilities
- competitive differentiation
- future-oriented platform improvements

All Wants SHALL NOT automatically be addressed.

A Want enters a strategic priority process.

The system MAY deliberately choose not to pursue it.

2.4 Need Versus Want Principle

If the system NEEDS to go faster, speed improvement may be urgent.

If the system only WANTS to go faster, speed improvement is optional and must
compete with other strategic priorities.

======================================================================
3. STRATEGIC PRIORITY REGISTER
======================================================================

All validated Needs and Wants SHALL enter a Strategic Priority Register.

Each item SHALL include:

- proposal ID
- title
- originating agent or department
- category: NEED or WANT
- need flag if applicable
- sponsoring department
- champion
- strategic rationale
- expected benefit
- measurable outcome
- cost estimate
- resource estimate
- risk
- dependencies
- security impact
- continuity impact
- financial impact
- user impact
- competitive impact
- recommended timing
- current priority score
- Board status
- implementation status

The Register SHALL be continuously maintained.

======================================================================
4. AGENT SELF-EVALUATION AND IDEA FORMATION
======================================================================

4.1 Protected Reflection Time

Every qualifying agent SHOULD receive scheduled time for:

- self-evaluation
- self-improvement reflection
- reviewing recurring friction
- identifying systemic limitations
- considering better ways of operating
- considering future opportunities

This time SHALL not be used to bypass normal operational responsibilities.

4.2 Individual Thinking Is Not Yet a Petition

An agent may think repeatedly about an improvement without immediately
placing a request into the strategic system.

This reduces noise and prevents the system from being flooded with premature
ideas.

4.3 Mentor and Trainer Discussion

Before a Want-based petition is submitted, the originating agent SHOULD
discuss the concept with:

- its trainer
- its mentor, where one exists
- relevant peer agents
- its departmental Evolution Liaison where appropriate

The purpose is to:

- improve the idea
- challenge assumptions
- identify duplicates
- determine whether the issue is already solved
- estimate value
- decide whether the idea is mature enough to petition

4.4 Petition Threshold

Only after discussion and refinement should the agent decide whether the idea
deserves a formal petition.

======================================================================
5. PETITION SYSTEM
======================================================================

5.1 Who May Petition

All authorized agents MAY originate petitions.

Departments MAY originate petitions.

Executives MAY originate petitions.

Security Defense, Business Continuity, Strategy, and Evolution MAY originate
petitions within their domains.

5.2 Petition Quality

A petition SHOULD include:

- problem or opportunity
- whether it appears to be Need or Want
- evidence
- expected benefit
- affected parties
- alternatives considered
- likely cost
- likely risk
- proposed sponsor
- supporting agents or departments

5.3 Petition Gate

Petitions SHALL pass through an intake filter to prevent spam, duplication,
low-quality requests, or unsupported speculation.

5.4 Petition Consolidation

Similar petitions SHOULD be consolidated into one strategic case where
possible.

======================================================================
6. CHAMPIONING
======================================================================

6.1 Champion

Every serious proposal SHALL have a Champion.

The Champion MAY be:

- originating agent
- department head
- designated representative
- coalition of agents
- Strategy-appointed sponsor

6.2 Champion Responsibilities

The Champion SHALL:

- explain the proposal
- defend its strategic value
- present evidence
- answer objections
- explain tradeoffs
- rally legitimate departmental support
- remain accountable for the proposal's stated objectives

6.3 Departmental Support

If a proposal gains strong support within a department, the department MAY
formally endorse it.

This endorsement increases visibility but does not guarantee approval.

======================================================================
7. STRATEGY ANALYSIS
======================================================================

The Strategy Department SHALL evaluate proposals across multiple dimensions.

Recommended dimensions:

- urgency
- necessity
- expected benefit
- strategic alignment
- financial impact
- competitive impact
- risk
- reversibility
- resource cost
- time to benefit
- opportunity cost
- implementation difficulty
- security implications
- continuity implications
- user value
- architectural fit
- dependency burden

No single numerical score SHALL automatically decide strategy.

======================================================================
8. COMMISSIONS
======================================================================

8.1 Purpose

For major proposals, Strategy MAY create a temporary Strategic Commission.

8.2 Commission Responsibilities

A Commission SHALL:

- investigate the proposal
- define scope
- collect evidence
- consult affected departments
- develop options
- estimate cost
- estimate risk
- produce recommendations
- define success criteria

8.3 Commission Independence

A Commission SHOULD not be composed entirely of agents already committed to
one outcome.

======================================================================
9. COMPETITIVE TENDER MODEL FOR IMPLEMENTATION OPTIONS
======================================================================

9.1 Principle

Where multiple implementation approaches are possible, Strategy MAY request
competing solution proposals.

This is analogous to a tender process.

9.2 Eligible Bidders

Bidders MAY include:

- internal agent teams
- architecture agents
- engineering agents
- provider-specific solution agents
- cross-department task forces

9.3 Tender Requirements

A proposal MAY be evaluated on:

- expected benefit
- cost
- reliability
- security
- continuity
- maintainability
- architectural simplicity
- implementation time
- reversibility
- provider dependence
- long-term adaptability

9.4 Selection

The most strategically appropriate solution SHALL be selected, not
necessarily the cheapest.

======================================================================
10. FUTURE-FOCUSED DEVELOPMENT PLANS
======================================================================

10.1 Purpose

The organization SHALL maintain periodically refreshed Future-Focused
Development Plans.

These plans turn selected Needs and Wants into an ordered roadmap.

10.2 Development Plan Contents

Each plan SHOULD include:

- active Needs
- active Wants
- strategic objectives
- approved initiatives
- priority order
- resource allocations
- expected milestones
- owners
- Evolution dependencies
- Board resolutions
- success measures

10.3 Periodic Refresh

Development Plans SHOULD be formally refreshed on a regular governance cycle.

======================================================================
11. EXTERNAL HORIZON INTELLIGENCE
======================================================================

11.1 Dedicated Branch

The Strategy Department SHALL maintain a separate Horizon Intelligence
Division.

Its mission is to continuously scan the external world for developments that
may affect MyAI.

11.2 Areas of Observation

The division MAY monitor:

- technology advances
- AI architecture
- new models
- agent frameworks
- security threats
- continuity practices
- infrastructure changes
- competitors
- market movement
- regulatory changes
- cost trends
- provider changes
- user expectations
- new opportunities
- industry standards

11.3 Strategic Purpose

The objective is not merely to react.

The objective is to keep MyAI several steps ahead where realistically
possible.

11.4 Relationship to Security Threat Intelligence

Security Threat Intelligence focuses on malicious threats.

Strategy Horizon Intelligence focuses on broader external change.

They SHALL exchange relevant information without duplicating authority.

12. FINANCIAL STRATEGY
======================================================================

The Strategy Department SHALL include long-term financial strategy capability.

It MAY develop strategies involving:

- cost structure
- infrastructure economics
- model-provider economics
- pricing
- margins
- capital allocation
- investment priorities
- revenue opportunities
- scenario planning
- resource optimization

Finance domain agents SHALL provide analysis.

Strategy SHALL integrate financial analysis with wider strategic objectives.

======================================================================
13. BOARD AND PRESIDENT
======================================================================

13.1 Human Governance

The Board and President are human governance roles.

The President SHALL preside over major Board sessions.

13.2 Mandatory Participants

Mandatory participants SHOULD include:

- Board President
- CEO
- major AI department heads
- Strategy Director
- Evolution Director
- Security Commander
- Business Continuity Commander
- other required executives

Other Board members MAY be human.

13.3 Day-to-Day Autonomy

The system SHOULD be designed so that the President does not need to manage
routine day-to-day operations.

Human attention SHOULD concentrate on:

- high-level governance
- major strategy
- constitutional issues
- exceptional risk
- major resource allocation
- Board decisions

======================================================================
14. WEEKLY BOARD / PARLIAMENTARY SESSION
======================================================================

14.1 Major Governance Event

The recurring Board session SHALL be treated as a major organizational event.

It is analogous to a parliamentary session or State-of-the-Union-style
governance forum.

14.2 Visibility

The session SHOULD have high internal visibility.

Agents MAY observe according to policy.

Departmental support and excitement may be visible.

14.3 Structured Agenda

Recommended agenda:

1. operating summary
2. security summary
3. continuity summary
4. strategic priority review
5. Need-based escalations
6. Want-based proposals
7. commission reports
8. agent champions
9. department endorsements
10. debate
11. voting
12. resolution adoption
13. Development Plan publication

======================================================================
15. CHAMPIONING AT BOARD SESSION
======================================================================

Agents and departments SHALL be allowed to champion qualified strategic
proposals.

A strong agent-originated idea MAY receive support from:

- peers
- trainers
- mentors
- department leadership
- allied departments

The proposing agent MAY present the idea directly or through an authorized
representative.

Support and enthusiasm MAY be visible.

However, popularity SHALL not replace evidence.

======================================================================
16. VOTING AND ELECTION OF PRIORITIES
======================================================================

16.1 Voting

Major strategic resolutions MAY be decided through Board voting.

16.2 AI Advisory Voting

AI departments MAY provide advisory up/down votes, rankings, or endorsements
to communicate system sentiment.

16.3 Human Authority

Human Board authority SHALL remain distinct from AI advisory support unless a
future constitutional amendment explicitly changes that model.

16.4 Priority Election

At the conclusion of the session, approved items SHALL be assigned strategic
priority.

======================================================================
17. BOARD RESOLUTIONS
======================================================================

Every approved resolution SHALL be:

- uniquely identified
- logged
- immutable in historical record
- assigned an owner
- assigned priority
- assigned milestones
- tracked
- linked to implementation
- reviewed until closure

High visibility creates high accountability.

======================================================================
18. DEVELOPMENT PLAN RELEASE
======================================================================

At the end of a major Board session, Strategy SHOULD publish the updated
Development Plan.

The plan SHALL reflect:

- approved resolutions
- priority order
- urgent Needs
- selected Wants
- resource allocation
- strategic commissions
- Evolution work authorized
- deferred items
- rejected items where appropriate

======================================================================
19. RELATIONSHIP WITH DEPARTMENT OF EVOLUTION
======================================================================

Strategy SHALL send approved systemic change to Evolution as an Evolution
Mandate.

The Mandate SHALL include:

- strategic rationale
- priority
- required outcomes
- Board resolution
- timing constraints
- resource envelope
- measurable success criteria
- need/want classification
- urgency flags

Evolution SHALL then design:

- directive
- training
- assimilation
- certification
- rollout
- rollback

======================================================================
20. RELATIONSHIP WITH COO
======================================================================

The COO SHALL coordinate operational execution of approved Strategy and
Evolution work.

The COO SHALL not independently rewrite strategic priorities.

======================================================================
21. RELATIONSHIP WITH CEO
======================================================================

The CEO SHALL provide executive coordination and organizational leadership.

The CEO SHALL attend major Board sessions.

The CEO MAY:

- sponsor strategic proposals
- challenge proposals
- request commissions
- report organizational state
- coordinate departments

24. ANTI-SPAM AND ANTI-NOISE RULES
======================================================================

The Strategy Department SHALL prevent petition overload.

Controls MAY include:

- reflection periods
- trainer/mentor pre-review
- duplicate detection
- minimum evidence
- petition quotas for low-value categories
- consolidation
- cooling periods
- rejection of vague or unserious requests

All agents have access to the petition system, but access does not imply an
unlimited right to consume strategic attention.

26. FOUNDATIONAL STRATEGY DIRECTIVES
======================================================================

DIRECTIVE S1
All systemic evolution SHALL pass through Strategy except emergency
containment actions.

DIRECTIVE S2
Strategy SHALL classify proposals as Need or Want.

DIRECTIVE S3
Needs receive priority according to severity and urgency.

DIRECTIVE S4
Wants are optional and compete for strategic priority.

DIRECTIVE S5
All Wants SHALL NOT be addressed.

DIRECTIVE S6
Every qualified agent MAY originate a strategic petition.

DIRECTIVE S7
Agents SHOULD first use self-reflection, trainer discussion, and mentor review
before petitioning.

DIRECTIVE S8
Major strategic proposals require evidence and a Champion.

DIRECTIVE S9
Major proposals MAY be investigated by commissions and competing solution
tenders.

DIRECTIVE S10
MyAI SHALL maintain Future-Focused Development Plans.

DIRECTIVE S11
A dedicated Horizon Intelligence Division SHALL monitor external change.

DIRECTIVE S12
Major strategy SHALL be debated in recurring Board sessions.

DIRECTIVE S13
The President, CEO, and major AI department heads SHALL attend required Board
sessions.

DIRECTIVE S14
AI agents and departments may champion, endorse, rank, and debate proposals.

DIRECTIVE S15
Board resolutions SHALL be logged, tracked, assigned, executed, and reviewed.

DIRECTIVE S16
The approved Development Plan SHALL be released after major Board sessions.

DIRECTIVE S17
Strategy decides WHAT and WHY; Evolution decides HOW systemic change is
assimilated.
