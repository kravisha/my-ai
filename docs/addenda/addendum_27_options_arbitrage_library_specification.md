FULL OPTIONS ARBITRAGE LIBRARY — ENGINEERING SPECIFICATION
Version 1.0 | 2026-08-21

PURPOSE
Build a deterministic, auditable system for detecting, validating, simulating, ranking and executing listed-options arbitrage.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE
A theoretical violation at mid prices is not executable arbitrage. A trade qualifies only after executable bid/ask prices, displayed/validated size, fees, funding, dividends, stock borrow, exercise/assignment, settlement, contract adjustments, slippage and operational constraints.

CLASSIFICATION
A = contractually locked after fills.
B = locked conditional on borrow/dividend/operational assumptions.
C = no-arbitrage violation with American exercise/assignment or settlement path complexity.
D = relative value/model signal, NOT arbitrage.

NOTATION
Sbid/Sask stock bid/ask; Cbid/Cask call bid/ask; Pbid/Pask put bid/ask.
K strike; T expiry; DF discount factor; PVK=K*DF.
PVDiv = PV of deterministic distributions; Fbid/Fask forward/futures prices; M multiplier.
European parity: C-P = S-PVDiv-PVK.
Forward form: C-P = DF*(F-K).
Every detector must internally use dated cash flows.

1. SHARED ARCHITECTURE
InstrumentMaster: underlying, root, venue, currency, style, strike, expiry, multiplier, settlement, deliverable, adjusted-contract lineage, tick, exercise cutoff.
MarketDataNormalizer: synchronized bids/asks/sizes/timestamps, condition codes, stale/crossed handling, NBBO/venue mode.
ReferenceData: borrow/lend curves, stock borrow, dividends, calendars, fees, taxes if configured, corporate actions.
CashflowEngine: dated currency-tagged cash flows.
ExerciseAssignmentEngine: American exercise, assignment, ex-dividend logic, expiry exercise and pin risk.
ConstraintEngine: parity, intrinsic, monotonicity, vertical, convexity, box, forward consistency and conditional calendar rules.
OpportunityEngine: executable package, capacity and worst-case net edge.
ExecutionPlanner: complex orders, leg sequencing, hedge/cancel/reprice rules.
RiskEngine: fill, liquidity, borrow, dividend, rate, assignment, settlement, corporate action and limits.
AuditStore: immutable inputs, quote IDs/times, formulas, assumptions, decisions, orders, fills and outcomes.

UNIVERSAL PIPELINE
1 Validate metadata/deliverable.
2 Validate quote freshness/integrity.
3 Select theorem appropriate to exercise/settlement style.
4 Build dated cash flows.
5 Calculate theoretical violation.
6 Reprice with executable sides.
7 Cap quantity by all legs, stock liquidity, borrow and limits.
8 Add funding, borrow, dividends, fees, slippage, exercise/settlement costs, configured taxes and buffer.
9 Run exercise/assignment/corporate-action scenarios.
10 Classify A/B/C/D.
11 Require positive worst-case net edge above absolute and return thresholds.
12 Revalidate immediately before release.
13 Execute atomically where possible.
14 Reconcile predicted vs realized cash flows.

2. ARBITRAGE LIBRARY

ARB-001 EUROPEAN PUT-CALL PARITY
C-P=S-PVDiv-PVK.
Rich-call direction: sell C at Cbid, buy P at Pask, buy stock at Sask. Pre-cost edge=Cbid-Pask-Sask+PVDiv+PVK.
Opposite: buy C at Cask, sell P at Pbid, short stock at Sbid. Pre-cost edge=-Cask+Pbid+Sbid-PVDiv-PVK, then explicit borrow/manufactured-dividend economics.
Require exact K/T/deliverable, European style, synchronized quotes, curve/dividend inputs. Reject stale data, adjusted mismatch and unavailable borrow.

ARB-002 CONVERSION
Package +stock +put -call. Terminal payoff K.
Executable cost=Sask+Pask-Cbid. Fair cost=PVK+PVDiv for deterministic distributions.
Gross edge=fair cost-executable cost. American version is separate because short-call assignment changes timing.

ARB-003 REVERSE CONVERSION
Package -stock -put +call. Terminal payoff -K.
Initial proceeds=Sbid+Pbid-Cask. Compare with PVK+PVDiv and subtract borrow, manufactured dividends, locate/recall reserve, funding asymmetry and costs. Confirmed shortability mandatory.

ARB-004 SYNTHETIC LONG FORWARD/STOCK
+call-put payoff S_T-K. European identity C-P+PVK=S-PVDiv.
Compare Cask-Pbid with executable prepaid stock/forward package. Only call arbitrage if opposite exposure and carry are locked.

ARB-005 SYNTHETIC SHORT FORWARD/STOCK
-call+put payoff K-S_T. Compare Cbid-Pask with actual short-stock/forward economics. Hard-to-borrow is a fair-value input.

ARB-006 EUROPEAN BOX
K1<K2: +C1 -C2 +P2 -P1. Terminal payoff K2-K1.
Long-box debit=C1ask-C2bid+P2ask-P1bid; compare with PV(width).
Short-box credit=C1bid-C2ask+P2bid-P1ask. Include funding/collateral/fees. American boxes route to ARB-018.

ARB-007 CALL VERTICAL BOUNDS
European: 0<=C1-C2<=DF*(K2-K1).
Violation if C2bid>C1ask+costs, or if C1bid-C2ask>PV(width)+costs.

ARB-008 PUT VERTICAL BOUNDS
European: 0<=P2-P1<=DF*(K2-K1). Use executable sides. American puts require immediate-exercise-aware bounds.

ARB-009 STRIKE CONVEXITY/BUTTERFLY
Equal spacing: C1-2C2+C3>=0, likewise puts.
Executable butterfly uses asks on wings and bids for two middle shorts. A credit for a nonnegative terminal payoff beyond costs is a candidate.
Unequal spacing: use correctly weighted slope monotonicity.

ARB-010 INTRINSIC/UPPER BOUNDS
European call lower=max(0,S-PVDiv-PVK), conventional upper S.
European put lower=max(0,PVK-(S-PVDiv)), conventional upper PVK under standard assumptions.
American call>=max(0,S-K); American put>=max(0,K-S).
Regenerate bounds from cash-flow dominance under negative rates/unusual products.

ARB-011 STRIKE MONOTONICITY
Calls nonincreasing in strike; puts nondecreasing.
Call violation: higher-K bid>lower-K ask+costs.
Put violation: lower-K bid>higher-K ask+costs.

ARB-012 MATURITY/CALENDAR CONSISTENCY
Never hard-code “longer expiry always costs more.” Dividends, rates, forwards, American exercise and settlement can invalidate it.
Apply only proven dominance rules. Otherwise classify as D diagnostic.

ARB-013 FORWARD/FUTURES VS SYNTHETIC FORWARD
European C-P=DF*(F-K). Synthetic long=Cask-Pbid; short=Cbid-Pask.
Adjust for premium funding, futures variation margin, forward/futures convexity when material, multiplier and settlement.

ARB-014 CASH-AND-CARRY
Buy underlying, finance, receive distributions, sell forward/future.
Reverse: short underlying, invest proceeds, buy forward, pay borrow/manufactured distributions.
Use dated carry cash flows, not blindly S*exp((r-q)T).

ARB-015 OPTION-IMPLIED DIVIDEND
PVDiv_implied=S-PVK-(C-P). Compare with declared/expected distributions.
Difference alone is not arbitrage; it can reflect borrow, funding, taxes, stale quotes or uncertainty. Elevate only with a complete locking package.

ARB-016 STOCK-BORROW/SYNTHETIC BASIS
Infer effective financing/borrow from parity and compare with executable stock borrow.
Borrow recall/repricing usually makes signals B/D rather than A.

ARB-017 AMERICAN CALL EARLY EXERCISE
Compare exercise-now versus hold using full cash flows. Before ex-dividend, captured dividend/funding benefit may exceed remaining optionality.
Short calls require assignment scenarios and resulting stock/dividend obligations.

ARB-018 AMERICAN BOX
Enumerate exercise/assignment states at economically relevant dates, especially ex-dividend and deep-ITM states.
Compute worst-case funding/inventory path. Class A only if every admissible contractual path preserves required profit.

ARB-019 AMERICAN PUT EARLY EXERCISE
Compare immediate K-S receipt with continuation, interest on K, dividends and optionality.
Short puts must model early assignment and resulting long-stock funding.

ARB-020 EXPIRATION/PIN RISK
Model automatic exercise rules, contrary instructions, cutoffs, after-hours underlying moves and residual stock.
Pin-risk packages are not A unless terminal handling removes uncertainty.

ARB-021 ADJUSTED CONTRACT/CORPORATE ACTION
Compare exact deliverables only. Value shares, cash, rights/baskets in adjusted contracts.
Reject parity against ordinary stock unless equivalence is proven. Unresolved corporate action is a hard stop.

ARB-022 MULTI-VENUE/COMPLEX-ORDER ARBITRAGE
Compare complex-book package with executable sum of legs/equivalent packages.
Include venue fees/rebates, routing latency, size and partial fills. Reject uncontrolled leg risk.

ARB-023 SYNTHETIC CASH/ZERO-COUPON
Use boxes or equivalent packages to synthesize deterministic future cash and infer lending/borrowing rates.
Balance-sheet, margin and collateral economics must be included before calling arbitrage.

ARB-024 CROSS-STRIKE DIGITAL/SLOPE CONSISTENCY
Call-spread slopes imply discounted state probabilities and must obey valid bounds.
Use finite-width executable packages; this generalizes vertical/convexity tests.

ARB-025 STATIC SURFACE ARBITRAGE
European surface must satisfy strike monotonicity, convexity and appropriate maturity consistency.
Normalize by forwards/DFs; identify violation; solve minimum-cost executable exploiting portfolio; classify only if actual bid/ask package locks payoff.
Odd fitted vols alone are D.

ARB-026 VOL-SURFACE RELATIVE VALUE
Smile/skew/model residuals. Always D unless independently converted into a static locked package. Requires model-risk and hedging statistics.

ARB-027 DISPERSION/CORRELATION
Index vs component options. Generally D because weights, correlation, rebalancing, dividends and volatility exposure remain.

ARB-028 CROSS-LISTING/ADR/ETF BASIS
Only eligible with executable conversion ratio, creation/redemption, FX, settlement, borrow and synchronized hours. Otherwise D.

ARB-029 FX/QUANTO/MULTI-CURRENCY PARITY
Include FX forwards, collateral currency and quanto terms. Never compare nominal cross-currency prices. A requires locked FX/settlement conversions.

ARB-030 NEGATIVE-RATE/ASYMMETRIC-FUNDING GENERALIZATION
Regenerate conventional bounds from discount factors/dominance. Support separate borrowing/lending and collateral curves. Framework rule for all detectors.

3. EXECUTION SPEC
Opportunity fields:
opportunity_id, detector_id, version, timestamp, instruments, sides, quantities, quote_ids, quote_timestamps,
theoretical_edge, executable_edge, all_costs, worst_case_net_edge, expected_net_edge, capital_required,
annualized_locked_return, capacity, classification, assumptions, hard_constraints, soft_risks,
interim_cashflows, expiry_cashflows, execution_plan, invalidation_rules.

Sizing: minimum executable capacity across all legs; account for multi-unit legs. Apply participation limits conservatively.
Atomicity preference: complex order; simultaneous IOC/FOK; bounded hedge-first/option-first; otherwise reject.
Revalidation: abort if edge, quote freshness, borrow, dividend/corporate action or halt state invalidates package.
Partial fills: recompute residual Greeks and worst-case unwind immediately; follow preapproved hedge/unwind tree.

4. COST MODEL
Mandatory: bid/ask, commissions, exchange/clearing/regulatory fees, borrow, locate, cash borrowing, cash lending,
collateral/margin funding, premium funding, dividends/manufactured dividends, exercise/assignment fees,
settlement, slippage/impact, configured tax model and safety buffer.
Never assume borrowing and lending occur at one risk-free rate.

5. DATA QUALITY HARD STOPS
Stale quote; unexplained crossed market; invalid executable bid; missing multiplier/style/deliverable;
mismatched settlement; unresolved corporate action; missing dividend state when material; missing borrow for short stock;
timestamp desynchronization; bad tick/outlier; execution-invalidating halt.

6. AMERICAN SCENARIO ENGINE
Identify relevant exercise dates; compare exercise/continuation for longs; enumerate plausible assignment states for shorts;
translate assignment into exact dated stock/cash inventory; apply funding/dividend/borrow; calculate worst admissible path.
A only if every contractual path preserves threshold profit.

7. TESTING
Unit: exact parity zero; adverse bid/ask cannot improve edge; added cost cannot improve edge; conversion payoff K;
European box payoff width; butterfly nonnegative; vertical bounded by width; adjusted mismatch rejected;
missing borrow rejected; stale quote rejected; negative-rate generalized bounds.
Property tests: generate arbitrage-free European chains, verify no executable positives; inject one violation and detect correct package.
Scenarios: dividend change/cancel; ex-div deep ITM call; deep ITM put/high rates; hard-to-borrow/recall; halts;
American box assignment; expiration pin; split/merger/special dividend; negative/asymmetric rates; partial fill;
quote update during execution; futures margin differences.
Replay: strictly event-time, no future-data leakage.

8. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
Production detector requires independently reviewed derivation; tested bid/ask mapping; symbolic/numeric cash-flow reconciliation;
explicit American applicability; explicit settlement/deliverable assumptions; mandatory costs; liquidity-constrained sizing;
fail-closed data quality; deterministic replay; reproducible audit; adversarial tests; schema-level separation of D from arbitrage.

9. CRITICAL REVIEW / SECOND-PASS CHECK
The following common errors were explicitly guarded against:
1 treating mid-price parity gaps as executable;
2 applying European parity to American options;
3 ignoring early assignment in American boxes/conversions;
4 ignoring discrete dividends or manufactured dividends;
5 assuming stock borrow exists or remains stable;
6 assuming one symmetric risk-free funding rate;
7 using naive calendar monotonicity;
8 ignoring negative rates;
9 mixing adjusted and standard deliverables;
10 ignoring physical vs cash settlement/timing;
11 ignoring futures variation margin/forward convexity;
12 ignoring multi-currency/FX cash flows;
13 treating volatility or dispersion relative value as risk-free;
14 ignoring size and multi-unit leg capacity;
15 ignoring partial-fill/legging loss;
16 using future information in historical replay;
17 ignoring expiration pin/contrary exercise instructions;
18 treating model-implied dividends/borrow as guaranteed cash flows;
19 omitting corporate-action hard stops;
20 calculating “profit” without worst-case dated cash-flow funding.

OPEN IMPLEMENTATION DECISIONS
These must be configuration, not hidden assumptions:
- supported exchanges/products;
- quote synchronization tolerance;
- stale thresholds by product/session;
- dividend confidence policy;
- borrow contractual model;
- lending/borrowing curves and collateral;
- fee/tax jurisdiction;
- minimum edge/return;
- slippage/impact model;
- atomic execution capabilities;
- American assignment scenario conservatism;
- treatment of after-hours expiration moves;
- capital/margin methodology.

10. CLAUDE/CODE IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT
Implement each ARB detector as a pure deterministic function over a versioned MarketSnapshot + ReferenceSnapshot.
Return either NoOpportunity(reason_codes[]) or Opportunity.
No detector may directly send orders.
Execution consumes only validated Opportunity objects and must independently revalidate.
All formulas must have unit tests and cash-flow tests.
Use decimal/fixed-point monetary arithmetic where practical; define rounding/tick rules centrally.
Version formulas, reference data and cost models so historical decisions remain reproducible.
Never silently substitute missing reference data with zero.
