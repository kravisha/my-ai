## Trainer agents, realistic synthetic scenarios, regression testing, historical replay, live evaluation, promotion gates, and agent health

### 1. Purpose

Testing is a first-class department, not an afterthought. The system must prove that agents perform their specified jobs, learn from mistakes, and remain reliable as conditions change.

### 2. Two Specialized Trainer Agents

- Explorer Trainer creates realistic option surfaces, peer relationships, term structures, skews, noise, and controlled volatility anomalies with known ground truth.
- Speculator Trainer creates realistic streams of social chatter, news-like context, rumors, noise, false leads, attention bursts, and corroborating or conflicting signals.
- Trainer agents are adversarial teachers. Their job is to expose weaknesses, not help production agents pass.
- Ground truth stays hidden from agents under test and is available to the validator.

### 3. Simulation Fidelity

- Generated data must become progressively closer to real market behavior.
- Simulation Fidelity is measurable: distributions, volatility regimes, skew and term structure, correlations, peer co-movement, noise, missingness, timing, and anomaly frequency should be compared with real data when available.
- Use seeded, reproducible scenarios so failures can be replayed exactly.
- Maintain obvious tests, subtle tests, and difficult boundary cases.
- Trainer quality is graded by realism, coverage, ability to expose defects, and predictive usefulness for later historical/live performance.

### 4. Initial Test Progression

1. Begin with one security and an easy known anomaly.
2. Repeat with two or three independent scenarios or securities to ensure the first success was not luck.
3. Expand to ten securities with explicit peer groups.
4. Test individual anomaly detection.
5. Test peer overlay and classification of common versus idiosyncratic events.
6. Test report creation, queue handoff, Analysis grading, confidence, and feedback.
7. Test resource and scaling behavior under starvation and backlog.
8. Add false positives, missing data, noisy surfaces, contradictory social evidence, and boundary thresholds.

### 5. Regression Framework

- Every fixed defect becomes a permanent regression scenario.
- Each release runs the full deterministic regression suite before promotion.
- Tests score detector correctness, report quality, queue behavior, attribution, confidence calibration, latency, resource behavior, and recovery.
- Every meaningful task performed during a test must be graded.
- A regression failure blocks promotion until resolved or explicitly waived with rationale.

### 6. Maturity Stages

**Stage 1 - Simulated Training**
- Use trainer-generated realistic data to teach and certify role correctness.
- No real-data purchase is required before the basic architecture and behavior prove themselves.

**Stage 2 - Historical Replay**
- After simulation certification, acquire real historical options data.
- Replay the past through the same provider interfaces and same production logic.
- Historical replay validates methodology against real market behavior rather than only implementation correctness.

**Stage 3 - Live Observation / Paper Research**
- Begin real-time ingestion in parallel with historical validation when practical.
- Produce live reports without automatic execution.
- Evaluate whether current-market opportunities are useful and whether data quality differs from historical or simulated feeds.

**Stage 4 - Production Promotion**
- Promote only capabilities that pass simulation, regression, historical validation, and required live-observation gates.
- Continue monitoring after promotion and roll back when performance materially regresses.

### 7. Historical Replay Clock

- Support a simulated clock where a historical period is replayed faster than wall time, for example one market day per hour.
- Agents receive only information that would have been available at that simulated timestamp.
- Prevent look-ahead leakage.
- Store replay run IDs, timestamps, data versions, agent/model versions, and outcomes for reproducibility.

### 8. Continuous Learning

- Learning is constant; deployment is gated.
- Analysis feedback improves Explorer and Speculator training priorities.
- Outcome scoring improves Analysis training and confidence calibration.
- Weaknesses discovered in historical or live evaluation become new trainer scenarios and permanent regressions.
- Production behavior changes only after passing the promotion pipeline.

### 9. Health and Refresh

- If an agent performs to expectation, do not restart it merely to imitate human rest.
- If performance degrades, investigate health signals including drift, resource accumulation, context bloat, repeated mistakes, and stale state.
- The COO may refresh, restart, retrain, quarantine, or replace an unhealthy process while preserving its durable knowledge and record.
