ORGANIZATION & AGENT ARCHITECTURE
Design Checkpoint — August 21, 2026

PURPOSE
Separate who acts, who advises, who evaluates, who governs, and who orchestrates.

1. CONTROLLER
The Controller is the central orchestration authority.
It:
- bootstraps the runtime;
- inspects system state;
- invokes schema/initialization mechanisms;
- controls lifecycle/data-world selection;
- ensures prerequisites exist before agents wake;
- presents appropriate services/data to agents.

Only the orchestration layer needs to understand Pre-Alpha, Alpha, Beta, and Live.

2. COO / OPERATIONAL ORCHESTRATION
The COO coordinates operational readiness and asks domain engines to certify whether required resources are usable.
Domain expertise remains with the domain engine.

Example:
The COO can ask whether usable simulated market data exists.
The Simulation Engine determines whether that simulated dataset is fit for simulation.

3. EXPLORER
Initial focus: volatility surfaces.

The Explorer:
- consumes assets from the Assets/Security Master;
- builds volatility surfaces;
- scans interesting stocks;
- detects structures, anomalies, or opportunities worth further investigation;
- collaborates with other discovery agents.

Early scope should remain deliberately narrow rather than attempting every possible strategy at once.

4. SPECULATOR
The Speculator consumes noisier information sources, including simulated chatter/noise during Pre-Alpha.
Its job is hypothesis generation and identification of things worth investigating, not final validation.

5. EXPLORER + SPECULATOR COLLABORATION
Explorer and Speculator collaborate to produce investigation candidates.
Their output is effectively:
“This is interesting enough to investigate.”

That candidate is delivered to the Analyst.

6. ANALYST
The Analyst performs deeper investigation on candidates.
It tests the thesis using the available market/reference/derived information and produces an analytical conclusion.

7. EVALUATOR
The Evaluator compares expected behavior/results against actual outcomes.

Expected → pass.
Unexpected → fail / investigation.

A failure triggers diagnosis:
- determine why the result differed;
- identify faulty assumptions, logic, data, models, or implementation;
- correct the problem;
- retry.

This is the central training loop.

8. TRAINING LOOP
Simulation Engine
→ synthetic world
→ Explorer + Speculator
→ investigation candidate
→ Analyst
→ decision/analysis
→ simulated outcome
→ Evaluator
→ PASS or FAIL
→ diagnosis/correction
→ retry

9. INTERFACE PRINCIPLE
Agents should not need to know whether data is simulated, historical, or live.
They consume stable domain interfaces.
The orchestration layer selects the underlying reality.

10. INITIAL MARKET RESEARCH FOCUS
The first Explorer research domain is volatility surfaces and related options relationships.

The working universe may emphasize “interesting stocks”:
- individually capable of meaningful volatility;
- collectively selected so that the basket can remain reasonably stable/diversified.

Put-call parity, volatility skew, term structure, and related pricing relationships are candidates for later formal strategy specifications after the underlying surfaces and data pipeline are trustworthy.
